Manifesto

The cat is out of the bag.

AI is here. It's in your inbox, your CRM and your competitor's office. Ignoring it doesn't slow it down.

But it can't do the things people actually come to you for. It can't earn trust. It can't care. It doesn't have taste.

So let it take the rest: the follow-ups, the data entry, the busywork that keeps you at the screen. Then go be good at what you do, out there, with people.

The more human you are, the more valuable you become.
